Default value = 2.6<br>DMEAN = median diameter of the particle number vs. diameter distribution (µm). Default value = 0.407 µm and SD = 4.0<br>SD = geometric standard deviation of the particle number vs. diameter distribution (dimensionless)<br>(I, sec. 2.1.6 and I, Appendix A)</td>

      <td>6p</td>
      <td>DNS, CAYM, EXPO, (8F10.0)</td>

    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>6t:1</td>
      <td>DIAM(1) FMASS(1)</td>

(IC(1) = 2 on card 2)<br>DIAM(I) = upper (i.e., larger particle) boundary diameter of the Ith particle size class<br>FMASS(I) = mass or activity fraction (depending on value of IC(5) on card 2) in the Ith particle size class<br>DIAM(NDSTR+1) = lower (i.e., smaller particle) boundary diameter of the NDSTRth particle size class</td>
    </tr>
